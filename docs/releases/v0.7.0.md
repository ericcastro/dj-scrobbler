# DJ Scrobbler 0.7.0

## Casting is experimental — macOS only

This release introduces **experimental TV casting on macOS** for Chromecast and AirPlay. It sends the focus view, player audio, and DJ Scrobbler overlays together to your TV.

**Windows and Linux casting support is coming soon.** Casting is disabled on those platforms in this release. The Windows and Linux apps remain available for playback, tracklists, recognition, and scrobbling.

Experimental casting may need several seconds to start and can vary with your TV and network. Use the TV button to connect or stop. AirPlay uses Apple's system picker.

## Added

- Timestamped YouTube descriptions and community comments as tracklist sources, alongside 1001Tracklists and set79.
- Background Shazam recognition when published tracklists are unavailable, with editable IDs and saved corrections.
- Focus-window controls, always-on-top playback, YouTube quality selection, and improved keyboard seeking.
- About → Licenses & sources, with bundled source archives, build recipes, and complete license texts.

## Improved

- Playback remains visible while tracklist providers work; provider switching and lookup progress are clearer.
- More reliable recognition, casting shutdown, connection recovery, and temporary-file cleanup.
- Manual track corrections retain their artwork lookup and cannot be overwritten by stale split candidates.
- YouTube cue rows stay in playback order, and missing timestamps remain distinct from a cue at zero.
- Tighter permission handling and external-link validation.
